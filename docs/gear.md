Gear I train and ride with around Katy and the greater Houston area — built for fitness skating, endurance cycling, and events like the MS150.

## Inline Skates

### EdgeTek carbon fiber skates

My primary speedskates for fitness and endurance sessions. The carbon shell keeps things light and responsive without sacrificing stability on longer pushes.

### FR1 325s (FR recreational)

A dependable recreational setup for mixed training — great for building leg strength, balance, and cardio with less impact than running. I pair these with my EdgeTek skates depending on the session and surface.

**Why skating for cross-training:** Inline skating builds leg strength and aerobic capacity without the pounding my hips take from high-mileage running — especially important after my 2019 total hip replacement.

## Upright Road Bike

### Trek Domane

My primary training bike for indoor Zwift sessions and outdoor miles. Fit matters more than components here — I've dialed in saddle height, fore-aft, and tilt to optimize hip stability and power transfer.

Post-THR, I focus extra attention on adductor and abductor strength to stay balanced and avoid compensations that show up on long rides.

## Recumbents

### Cruzbike Vendetta V20

The yellow V20 is my go-to for event day and long endurance efforts where comfort and efficiency matter most. Equipped with Dura-Ace R9000 components.

**Drivetrain update:** I ran a 53/39 for a while but have switched to an AbsoluteBlack oval 52/36 setup. It looks great and I expect it to improve the hill experience on longer rides.

### HP Velotechnik Scorpion fs26

The fs26 is a Scorpion by HP Velotechnik — a full-suspension recumbent that rides smooth and handles varied terrain well. A different feel from the V20, but another great option in the recumbent lineup.

## Training Setup

- **Indoor:** Zwift on the trainer through the winter base-building phase
- **Recovery:** Cold plunges, sauna sessions, and intentional fueling around training blocks
- **Cross-training:** Heavy use of inline skating between cycling blocks to keep fitness up without overloading the hips
