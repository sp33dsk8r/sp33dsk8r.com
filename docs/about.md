**Bryan “sp33dsk8r” Taylor** is a passionate inline speedskater, endurance cyclist, and lifelong fitness enthusiast based in Katy, Texas (Houston metro area). Bryan brings remarkable resilience and data-driven discipline to his training — especially after a full hip replacement in 2019 — while staying deeply committed to pushing personal limits through movement.

## Skating

A dedicated recreational and fitness skater, he regularly trains on **FR** recreational skates (with 125mm/110mm wheel setups) and his **EdgeTek** carbon fiber skates. Bryan is meticulous about gear optimization, from frame tuning and bumper repairs to clipless pedal positioning for efficient power transfer. He blends high-volume outdoor sessions with cross-training that builds both endurance and agility.

## Cycling

On two wheels, Bryan is equally at home on his **Cruzbike Vendetta V20** recumbent (with Dura-Ace setup and ongoing crank/chainring upgrades for climbing efficiency), his **Trek Domane** upright road bike, and **Riivo** carbon fiber cycling skates for low-impact cross-training. He has completed multiple **MS150** rides (his 6th in 2026) supporting MS research, a cause close to his heart due to family connections. His training is structured and tech-forward: Garmin Fenix/Edge ecosystem, power meters, Zwift indoor sessions, cold plunges, contrast therapy, and careful nutrition protocols (especially while on his body recomposition journey targeting ~13% body fat while preserving muscle).

Bryan believes strongly in the transformative power of consistent movement for physical health, mental clarity, and longevity. He enjoys sharing practical knowledge — gear tweaks, training insights, recovery strategies, and n=1 experimentation — with the Houston-area skating and cycling communities. Whether lacing up skates for a neighborhood session, logging long miles on the Vendetta, or mentoring others on efficient form and equipment, he’s all about community, continuous improvement, and proving that age is just a number when you stay committed.

Active, analytical, and always tinkering, Bryan “sp33dsk8r” embodies the joy of lifelong athletic pursuit in the Texas heat.
